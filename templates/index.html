THE PROJECT

Meet NutriAI.

NutriAI is an AI-powered nutrition assistant built to help users explore food ideas, meal planning and basic wellness calculations. It uses a Flask backend and IBM watsonx.ai to generate responses based on users' questions and saved preferences.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECT
